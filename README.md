### About me

I am a **Product Owner at i-Yusr**, the company behind **QivoPay**, a consumer-credit platform in Algeria.
I started on the business side as a credit analyst, became Credit Analyst Lead, and moved into product.
That path means I know the users, the operations and the risk behind the features I build.

### What I do

- **Product ownership** — lead a cross-functional Scrum team delivering web, mobile and ERP platforms for clients in education, finance and business management
- **Fintech workflows** — design end-to-end credit processing workflows with partner banks and points of sale
- **Product discovery** — set up an internal intake process with Jira Product Discovery to collect, prioritise and track needs
- **Data & finance** — define reporting needs and categorisation for an in-house finance solution built on Power BI
